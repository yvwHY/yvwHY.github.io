Research & selected projects

2026
Solo Choir — MA thesis · real-time vocal instrument · Goldsmiths
Turns one singer into a choir: sing a phrase and the system answers with harmony voices re-synthesised in the singer's own voice, using real-time neural voice conversion on CPU.
Tabletop responsive instrument (four speakers, head model, boom microphone) shown with two wearable prototypes: an open-ear bone-conduction headband and an auxetic neck ring for feeling harmony in the body.
demo: vimeo.com/1223687812  ·  code: github.com/yvwHY/solo-choir  ·  case study
2026
Fabri-Form — robotics · pattern cutting · Goldsmiths
A robotic pattern-cutting interface: a multimodal LLM reads a hand-drawn sketch, generates 2D cutting paths, and a Raspberry Pi Pico-driven laser traces them onto fabric.
2025
The Bonnet — wearable · computer vision · Goldsmiths
An anti-surveillance headpiece: a custom-trained YOLOv8 model detects a CCTV camera and an Arduino servo drops a fabric veil over the face.
2025
Gesture-Float — hardware · wireless control · Goldsmiths
A wireless flying unit steered by the arm: an arm-mounted Pico W streams hand pitch and roll over Wi-Fi (UDP) to a helium balloon unit, on hand-soldered circuits.
