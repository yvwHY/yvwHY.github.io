Profile

I design the body as an interface — wearable instruments, embodied sound, and real-time AI systems that let a person extend, feel, and learn through their own body. My path runs from fashion pattern-cutting (UAL) through generative-AI media to computational arts, grounded in professional visual-design practice in Taipei (2020–25). Current focus: Solo Choir, a wearable instrument that turns one singer into a choir.

Selected projects

2026
Solo Choir — MA thesis · wearable instrument
Turns one singer into a choir: diatonic SATB harmony re-synthesised in the singer's own voice, delivered into the body via open-ear bone conduction (learning) and performed from it as a simultaneous chord (surface exciters). Real-time neural voice conversion on CPU, ~47 ms. Research question: does sending harmony into / from the body change how singers learn and perform it, versus harmony mediated by screens or speakers? case study →
neural voice conversion · real-time DSP · bone conduction · hardware prototyping
2026
Choir of Three (and One) — sound / AI
A DDSP model trained on ~10 minutes of my own singing performs three parts of a Bach chorale; the AI yields to silence when I hold my own part — a study in mutual yielding as the core of choral practice.
DDSP · Tone.js · ml5.js · p5.js
2026
Fabri-Form — robotics
A robotic interface for pattern-cutting: a multimodal LLM reads a hand-drawn sketch, generates 2D cutting paths, and a Pico-driven laser traces them onto fabric.
multimodal LLM · Raspberry Pi Pico · laser
2025
The Bonnet — wearable
An anti-surveillance headpiece: a custom-trained YOLOv8 model spots a CCTV camera and a servo drops a fabric veil over the face — reclaiming the gaze.
YOLOv8 · OpenCV · Arduino
2025
Gesture-Float — hardware
A wireless flying unit steered by the arm: an arm-mounted Pico W streams hand pitch and roll over Wi-Fi to a helium balloon unit, hand-soldered for stable, organic flight.
Pico W · UDP · PCB · soldering
2025
Draping Duet — performance
A real-time audiovisual installation reframing fashion draping as choreography — StreamDiffusion turns the atelier into flowing silk while a microphone amplifies the friction of the cloth.
StreamDiffusion · TouchDesigner · OBS
